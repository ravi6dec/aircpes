Publication

Selected accepted and presented papers will be considered for Springer publication opportunities, subject to the applicable Springer editorial and peer-review processes.

  • At least one author of every accepted paper must register for and present the paper at the conference; otherwise the paper will not be included in the proceedings.
  • Each paper may be a maximum of 8 pages.
  • Papers are selected through double-blind peer review; at least two independent reviewers must recommend acceptance.
