1st International Conference on

Artificial Intelligence for Resilient Cyber-Physical Energy Systems

AIRCPES 2027

Organised by Maharaja Agrasen Institute of Technology (MAIT), Delhi, India
in collaboration with Birmingham City University (BCU), UK

8th – 9th March, 2027  ·  Hybrid Mode

Call for Paper

Researchers, academics, scientists, engineers and industry professionals are invited to submit original and unpublished research papers related to the conference theme. Selected accepted and presented papers will be considered for Springer publication opportunities, subject to the applicable Springer editorial and peer-review processes.

Conference Topics

The conference welcomes original research papers covering, but not limited to:

  • AI and Machine Learning for Energy Systems
  • Smart Grids, Microgrids and Cyber-Physical Energy Systems
  • Resilient and Secure Energy Systems
  • Cybersecurity and Cyber-Resilience
  • Digital Twins and Predictive Maintenance
  • Renewable Energy and Energy Integration
  • Energy Storage and Battery Management
  • Second-Life Batteries and Battery Health Assessment
  • Electric Vehicles, Smart Charging and Battery Swapping
  • AI-Based Energy Forecasting and Optimisation
  • Intelligent Energy Management Systems
  • IoT, Sensors and Edge Computing for Energy
  • Fault Detection, Protection and Intelligent Control
  • Distributed Energy Resources and Virtual Power Plants
  • Energy Efficiency and Smart Buildings
  • Smart Cities and Integrated Energy Systems
  • AI for Rural and Decentralised Energy Systems
  • Explainable and Trustworthy AI for Energy Applications
  • Emerging AI Technologies and Future Energy Systems

Conference Tracks

Track 1

Artificial Intelligence and Data-Driven Energy Systems

Artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, generative AI, computational intelligence, big data analytics, data-driven modelling, optimisation, forecasting, decision-making and intelligent control for modern energy systems, including explainable and trustworthy AI, federated and privacy-preserving learning, physics-informed machine learning, transfer learning, edge AI/TinyML, and open datasets and benchmarks for energy AI.

Track 2

Cyber-Physical Energy Systems, Smart Grids and Microgrids

Cyber-physical energy systems, smart grids, intelligent microgrids, distributed energy resources, distributed generation, energy management, demand response, virtual power plants, real-time monitoring and control, grid automation, system stability and intelligent coordination of interconnected energy assets, including AI for power system state estimation, power flow, unit commitment, congestion management and transmission/distribution planning; AI-based control of grid-forming and grid-following inverters, low-inertia grids, HVDC/FACTS and power quality; and AI for island, remote, off-grid and disaster-relief microgrids.

Track 3

Resilience, Reliability and Cybersecurity

Energy system resilience, reliability, cyber-physical security, cyber-threat detection, intrusion detection, anomaly detection, cybersecurity risk assessment, fault detection and diagnosis, self-healing systems, intelligent protection and control, disaster-resilient infrastructure and secure-by-design energy systems, including adversarial robustness of AI models, secure AI-enabled communication (5G/6G, IoT, satellite networks) and AI for extreme-weather and climate-driven outage prediction.

Track 4

Renewable and Sustainable Energy Systems

Artificial intelligence for renewable energy integration, solar photovoltaic systems, agrivoltaics, wind and hybrid renewable energy systems, renewable energy forecasting, resource assessment, energy efficiency, sustainable energy planning, decentralised and rural energy systems, energy access and climate-resilient energy infrastructure, including AI for hydro, geothermal, biomass, waste-to-energy and ocean energy; AI for green hydrogen, electrolysers, fuel cells and power-to-X; AI for carbon accounting, emissions tracking, CCUS and life-cycle assessment; and the AI-driven energy-water-food nexus and energy harvesting.

Track 5

Energy Storage, Batteries and Electric Mobility

Battery energy storage systems, battery management systems, state-of-charge and state-of-health estimation, battery degradation and remaining useful life prediction, second-life batteries, battery health grading and assessment, electric vehicles, smart EV charging, battery swapping, vehicle-to-grid (V2G), vehicle-to-building (V2B) and intelligent coordination of storage and electric mobility, including thermal and hydrogen storage, AI-based charging infrastructure planning, wireless EV charging, and electrification of heavy-duty, rail, maritime and aviation transport.

Track 6

Digital Twins, Predictive Maintenance and Intelligent Energy Infrastructure

Digital twins for energy systems, AI-enabled predictive maintenance, condition monitoring, asset management, intelligent diagnostics, IoT-enabled monitoring, intelligent sensors, edge and cloud computing, system modelling and simulation, virtual commissioning and AI-based operational decision support, including AI for thermal systems (district heating and cooling, heat pumps, CHP), industrial energy systems and process-industry efficiency, and interoperability standards (IEC 61850, OpenADR, IEEE 2030) for AI-enabled infrastructure.

Track 7

Intelligent Energy Markets, Smart Buildings and Smart Cities

AI applications in energy markets, electricity pricing and trading, peer-to-peer energy trading, blockchain-enabled energy transactions, prosumers and energy communities, demand-side flexibility, smart homes and buildings, building energy management, smart cities, integrated energy systems, intelligent mobility and data-driven energy services, including smart metering and AMI analytics, non-intrusive load monitoring (NILM), consumer load profiling, multi-energy and sector-coupled systems, AI for market design and regulation, and consumer behaviour, social acceptance and energy justice.

Track 8

Emerging Technologies, Applications and Case Studies

Emerging AI technologies and applications in energy systems, generative AI and large language models for energy, AI for climate adaptation and resilience, industry and utility applications, AI data centres integration, pilot and demonstration projects, community and rural energy applications, smart city deployments, interdisciplinary case studies, field demonstrations, lessons learned and future directions, including agentic AI, foundation models for energy, quantum and neuromorphic computing for energy applications, energy-efficient and sustainable AI (the energy footprint of AI itself), and AI governance, ethics and regulation in the energy sector.
