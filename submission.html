Submission Guidelines

  • Only original papers that have not been published or submitted for publication elsewhere will be considered.
  • Manuscripts should be submitted in PDF format, with a maximum length of 8 pages.
  • The paper should not contain page numbers or any special headers or footers.
  • Review is double-blind: the list of authors must not be included in the manuscript PDF.
  • Papers that do not conform to the double-blind submission policy will be rejected without review.
  • Changes to the paper metadata on Microsoft CMT are permitted before the paper submission deadline.
  • Papers outside the scope of the conference will be desk-rejected without being sent for peer review.

Review Process

All submitted papers will be judged on their originality, technical and/or research content and depth, correctness, relevance to the conference, contribution and readability. Each paper will be sent to at least three reviewers, and recommendations of at least two reviewers are needed for acceptance. The final decision on acceptability for publication lies with the conference chair.

Plagiarism Policy

The organisers regard plagiarism as serious professional misconduct. All submissions will be screened with Turnitin plagiarism detection software, and the similarity index must be below 15%. When plagiarism is identified, the submissions by the same author will be rejected. Self-plagiarism and dual submission are also treated as misconduct.
