About the Conference

To cater to the grid transformation driven by the rapid deployment of renewable and distributed energy resources, energy storage and electric vehicles, conventional power grids are transitioning from traditional, infrastructure-centric systems to cyber-physical systems. Rising electricity demand, decarbonisation targets, the transition of conventional consumers to active prosumers, and the requirement of highly secure communication interfaces create increasingly complex operating environments.

Modern energy infrastructure is fundamentally a cyber-physical system, in which physical assets such as generation units, transmission and distribution networks, microgrids, batteries, electric vehicles and smart appliances are closely integrated with sensing, communication, computing and control layers. This integration creates significant opportunities for artificial intelligence while simultaneously introducing new vulnerabilities. AI can turn the large volumes of data generated by SCADA systems, smart meters, phasor measurement units, IoT sensors and distributed energy resources into actionable information — supporting forecasting, fault detection, anomaly identification, state estimation, stability assessment, condition monitoring, predictive maintenance, energy optimisation and real-time decision-making.

AIRCPES 2027 provides a platform for contributions from both the electrical engineering and computer science communities, encouraging interdisciplinary research that connects power systems, AI, communications, computing, cybersecurity and data science. By bringing together theoretical developments, computational methods, experimental studies, practical deployments and real-world case studies, the conference highlights how AI can contribute to more intelligent, secure, adaptive, resilient and sustainable cyber-physical energy systems.

The conference is organised by Maharaja Agrasen Institute of Technology (MAIT), Delhi, India, in collaboration with Birmingham City University (BCU), UK, under the IEEE Smart Cities initiative. This is the first edition of the conference, and about 200 participants are expected.
