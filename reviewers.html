Call for Reviewers – AIRCPES 2027

The 1st International Conference on Artificial Intelligence for Resilient Cyber-Physical Energy Systems (AIRCPES 2027) invites nominations from academicians, researchers, industry professionals and domain experts to join the Reviewer Panel for the conference.

Why Join as a Reviewer?

  • Contribute to maintaining the quality of conference publications
  • Review innovative research from global researchers
  • Network with academicians and industry experts
  • Enhance your professional and research profile
  • Stay at the forefront of AI, smart grids and resilient energy systems research

Submit your paper via Microsoft CMT

Before submitting, please check that your manuscript meets the requirements:

  • Anonymised PDF — no author names, affiliations or e-mails in the manuscript (double-blind review).
  • Length & format — maximum 8 pages, PDF, without page numbers or special headers/footers.
  • Originality — unpublished work; Turnitin similarity index below 15%.
